Privacy Policy

Pocket Bento (the "app")
Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information the app uses and why. We keep it short because the app collects very little.

What the app stores on your device

Your game progress, coins, purchases and settings are saved on your device. We do not run accounts or servers for the game, and we cannot see this information.

Ads

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads and measure how they perform, Google and its ad partners may collect information from your device, such as your advertising identifier, IP address, device type, and how you interact with ads. If you allow tracking when the app asks, ads may be better matched to your interests. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track," ads are still shown but are not personalized across other apps and websites. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings.

See Google's own explanation of how it uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In some regions, such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before showing ads that use your data. You can change your choice later where the app offers that option.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment details.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, contact list, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. We do not sell your personal information.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off tracking for the app in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Reset your device's advertising identifier in Settings, Privacy & Security, Apple Advertising or Tracking.
  • Delete the app to remove the data saved on your device.

If you live somewhere with privacy laws that give you additional rights, you can contact us with questions or requests at the address below.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
