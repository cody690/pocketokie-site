Bento Packing Puzzle: Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email us at pocketokie@gmail.com and tell us your device and what happened.

Common questions

How do I turn a piece? Tap it, in the tray or in the box.

I'm stuck. Use the lightbulb for a hint, the arrow to undo your last piece, or the circle arrow to restart the level.

I bought "Remove ads" but still see an ad. Open the shop, tap "Restore purchases," then restart the game. The optional reward ads you choose to watch for hints and bonus coins always stay.

I lost my progress. Progress is saved on your device. If you delete the app or switch phones, it can't be recovered yet.
